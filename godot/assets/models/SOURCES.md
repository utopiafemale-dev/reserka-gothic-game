# 3D scenery provenance

The eight GLB meshes were generated specifically for Reserka with the original
procedural Blender script `godot/tools/build_3d_models.py`. Geometry and material
colors are generated in that script; no downloaded model or texture pack is used.
Stone surface variation is procedural noise in `three_d/main.gd`.

The 2.5D characters retain the existing Gothicvania knight and enemy sprite
sheets. The Adventurer sheets were copied from the repository's existing
Adventurer assets. Spectral/Crimson are palette variants of the knight.
These inherited sheets remain under their respective original licenses.
